## Structural findings worth carrying forward

1. **Power and compute have merged into one investment theme.** ArcLight being bought by DigitalBridge,
   Knighthead's Homer City, Engine No. 1 with Chevron, Five Point's PowerBridge, ADQ with Energy Capital
   Partners, and Brookfield's Bloom Energy framework are all the same trade expressed differently.
2. **Private capital displaced the bank market at the top end in 2025.** The Meta/Blue Owl/PIMCO
   USD 27bn+ private placement is larger than any syndicated data center loan in the dataset.
3. **Insurance general-account money is arriving through affiliated managers** (MetLife IM directly;
   Athene via Apollo; Global Atlantic via KKR) rather than as named investors on deals — which is why it
   is systematically under-counted in sector lists.
4. **One institution is often three counterparties.** Citizens, Blue Owl, Ares, Macquarie, Blackstone,
   Starwood and BlackRock each transact through multiple separately branded teams. The table preserves
   those distinctions where they matter for outreach.
