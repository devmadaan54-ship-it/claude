Where these come from, and how to search them yourself

The free portals are enough to find everything on this page. The catch is captchas and expiring links, so the working paths are written down here.

CPPP · eprocure.gov.in

Every central ministry, CPSE and autonomous body must publish here. Keyword search is captcha-gated, but these list pages are not:

  • /cppp/latestactivetendersnew/cpppdata — about 29,600 live central tenders, 10 per page.
  • /cppp/gemtender — a mirror of roughly 39,000 live GeM bids with real GEM/2026/B numbers, buyer and close date. Sorted oldest first, so the newest bids sit on the last pages.
  • /cppp/tendersclosingbydays/bytoday and /cppp/highvaluetenders.
  • Pagination is not ?page=N; it is ?url= followed by the base64 of the full URL including ?page=N.
  • Detail links carry a timestamp and expire. Keep the tender ID and re-open it through "Search with Tender ID".

GePNIC · eprocure.gov.in/eprocure and etenders.gov.in

Same NIC software on both, and on most state portals. The advanced-search result page can be opened without a captcha:

  • /eprocure/app?component=%24DirectLink&page=FrontEndAdvancedSearchResult&service=direct — 20 rows per page; 129 pages on eprocure, 97 on etenders.
  • "Tenders by Organisation" avoids the captcha too and is the fastest way to watch a specific buyer.
  • Bidder enrolment is free. You need a Class 3 organisation DSC with signing and encryption; a signing-only certificate cannot open the bid packet.

GeM · gem.gov.in

Mandatory channel for most central IT-services purchases. The bid site blocked automated access from our environment, which is why bids above were read from the CPPP mirror instead.

  • Register as a services seller, link your Udyam and DPIIT numbers, and list in the right service categories. Bid notifications then come by category.
  • Buyers can tick "Startup exemption" and "MSE exemption" on a bid, which waives turnover and experience. Look for that line in every bid document.
  • Direct purchase up to ₹50,000 and L1 among three sellers up to ₹10 lakh happen with no bid at all. That is where first references come from.

State portals

Each state runs its own instance and needs a separate free enrolment with the same DSC.

  • Maharashtra mahatenders.gov.in, Delhi govtprocurement.delhi.gov.in, UP etender.up.nic.in, Tamil Nadu tntenders.gov.in, Kerala etenders.kerala.gov.in, West Bengal wbtenders.gov.in, MP mptenders.gov.in, Odisha tendersodisha.gov.in, Rajasthan eproc.rajasthan.gov.in, Punjab eproc.punjab.gov.in, Haryana etenders.hry.nic.in.
  • Karnataka kppp.karnataka.gov.in, Telangana tender.telangana.gov.in and Gujarat nprocure.com are not NIC instances and have their own registration.
  • On NIC instances, "Tenders by Organisation" and the tender view pages open without a captcha; that is how the state rows above were read, about 50,000 active tenders across 11 portals.
  • Telangana's portal needs a real browser (scripted requests are rejected), Karnataka's is a JavaScript app with a captcha-backed search, and Gujarat's nprocure did not respond. Most Gujarat IT buys are on GeM under Gujarat Informatics Ltd anyway.
  • State IT agencies (MahaIT, UPDESCO, UPLC, KEONICS, Gujarat Informatics, TSTS, MPSEDC) run their own empanelments, and "Limited" tenders on the state portals then go only to empanelled vendors. That is the friendliest route for a new firm.

PSU and institutional portals

  • Railways buy through IREPS ireps.gov.in, which needs its own vendor registration.
  • ITI Limited, TCIL and RailTel regularly float "business associate" and "backend partner" EOIs on CPPP and etenders. They win the big state contract and sub-contract the build.
  • NICSI publishes empanelments for software, cloud and manpower; its June 2026 AI-modernisation empanelment reserved ten of twenty slots for startups and MSMEs.
  • Banks (PNB, Union Bank, Punjab & Sind) run "Hiring of Agency for IT Projects" bids on GeM nearly every week, but usually ask for BFSI references.

What could not be reached

  • bidplus.gem.gov.in and gem.gov.in reset the connection to automated clients. Use the CPPP mirror or a logged-in browser.
  • bidassist.com and tendersontime.com return 403 to non-browser traffic.
  • tenders.gov.in no longer resolves; it was replaced by CPPP years ago.
  • TendersInfo shows no tender rows without a login.

Getting bid-ready

The rules that actually help a firm with no history, and what it costs to be able to submit a bid at all. About three to five weeks and under ₹40,000 for the minimum set.

The relaxations you will lean on

  • GFR 2017 Rule 173(i): prior turnover and experience may be relaxed for DPIIT-recognised startups. Discretionary, so it only helps when the bid document says so. Raise a pre-bid query citing it when it is missing.
  • GFR Rule 170: EMD is not taken from MSEs or DPIIT startups. Performance security of 3 to 5 percent is still due on award.
  • MSE Procurement Order 2012: central buyers must source 25 percent from MSEs. An MSE within 15 percent of L1 can match and take 25 percent of the order. Udyam registration is the only proof needed.
  • Make in India Order (revised July 2024): an Indian services firm is normally Class-I local. Declare local content in every bid.
  • DPIIT startup definition (Feb 2026): turnover cap ₹200 crore, ten-year window. Proprietorships do not qualify, so incorporate as Private Limited or LLP.
  • GeM slabs: direct purchase up to ₹50,000, L1 among three up to ₹10 lakh, bids above that. Caution money was discontinued in early 2026.

Checklist, in order

  1. Incorporate as Private Limited. Keep the legal name identical on every later registration.
  2. PAN, TAN, current account, GST (three working days with Aadhaar authentication).
  3. Udyam registration as a Micro services enterprise. Free, instant, tick the GeM linkage.
  4. DPIIT Startup India recognition. Write the innovation note as a product story, not "we do IT services".
  5. Class 3 organisation combo DSC (signing plus encryption) for each signatory, on a USB token.
  6. GeM seller registration; link Udyam and DPIIT; list service categories.
  7. Bidder enrolment on CPPP, then on each state portal you will bid.
  8. A standing bid pack: certificates, CA-certified turnover and net worth, board resolution, non-blacklisting affidavit, local-content declaration, CV templates.
  9. Register with the Telangana, Karnataka, Gujarat and Maharashtra startup cells for state-level waivers and pilot orders.
  10. ISO 9001 in year one, ISO 27001 by month nine. Defer CMMI Level 3 until you have three completed projects.

State routes worth registering for

  • Telangana: GO 08 of 2018 waives EMD, turnover and experience for startups, gives a 15 percent price advantage, and lets you propose a pilot by EOI to the startup cell.
  • Karnataka: 2025-30 policy gives startups no EMD, limited tenders up to ₹50 lakh for empanelled startups, "Government First" pilot orders up to ₹25 lakh, and a Startup Inclusion Plan that rewards big SIs for sub-contracting at least 10 percent to a startup.
  • Gujarat: 2018 resolution removed experience, turnover, tender fee and EMD for startups in state procurement.
  • Maharashtra: 2018 policy exempts state-registered startups from experience and turnover with L1 plus 15 percent matching. The 2025 policy's procurement clauses could not be confirmed.
  • Uttar Pradesh: UPDESCO and Uptron run recurring startup empanelments for app and website development, one of which is on this page.

Why bids get thrown out

  • A price anywhere in the technical cover.
  • EMD or exemption certificate missing, wrong beneficiary, or expired.
  • Turnover for the wrong financial years, or unaudited statements.
  • "Similar work" that does not match the RFP's own definition.
  • Entity name that differs across PAN, GST, Udyam and work orders.
  • Corrigenda not incorporated, or the bid never frozen before the deadline. Server time is IST.

How a new firm gets past the turnover wall

  • Bid GeM tenders where the startup or MSE exemption is ticked, and take the ₹50,000 and ₹10 lakh no-bid orders to build completion certificates.
  • Answer the ITI, TCIL, RailTel and NICSI partner EOIs. The PSU wins the contract; you deliver.
  • Get on state empanelments early. Each is a three-year licence to receive work orders without a fresh tender.
  • Consortium or JV only where the RFP allows it; sub-contract experience does not count toward your own eligibility.
