# Government tech tenders snapshot, 14 September 2026

Research pack for a new Indian tech services firm looking for government work.

- `tenders.csv`: 147 open tenders (110 read from official portals, 37 aggregator leads), with closing date, fit tier, value, EMD, ID and source URL.
- `report.html`: the same data as a filterable page, plus portal how-tos, paid alert service comparison, bid-readiness checklist, and international portals.
- `eligibility-notes.md`: the rules that help a new firm (GFR 173(i), Rule 170 EMD waiver, MSE order, DPIIT 2026 definition, GeM slabs) and costs to become bid-ready.
- `aggregators-and-international-notes.md`: paid service pricing and international portal findings.
- `portal-search-notes.md`: captcha-free URLs on CPPP and GePNIC, and GeM mirror pagination.

Fit tiers are a judgement from the listing text. Closing dates move by corrigendum; verify on the source portal before bidding.
